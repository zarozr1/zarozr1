Your phone screen reacts to the lightest brush of your finger, and ignores a pen pressed as hard as you can. So what is it actually feeling? Under the glass sits a grid of tiny wires, charged with electricity. Your finger is full of water, so it pulls a little of that charge away. The phone finds the exact spot where the charge dropped. A pen is plastic. It steals nothing, so the screen never knows it's there. Follow for the next one.
